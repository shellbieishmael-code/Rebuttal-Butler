REBUTTAL BUTLER
====================

Start
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (the Desktop is fine) and keep its contents together.
  2. Double-click RebuttalButler.exe.
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     (That message appears for any app that is not digitally signed.)
  3. On the Settings tab, under "Audio sources", leave the source on Desktop audio: this PC's
     speakers (recommended).
  4. Take your call in the browser dialer as usual. Click "Start Listening".
     Suggested rebuttals pop up when the customer raises an objection.

Good to know
  - It transcribes what this PC plays (the customer, through your speakers or headset).
    Your own microphone is picked under "My microphone" on the Settings tab and is used for recording your
    side of the call. Your side is not transcribed.
  - No VB-Cable, OBS or internet connection is needed.
  - "Test Audio" listens for 3 seconds and tells you whether sound is coming through.
    Windows sends nothing while the PC is silent, so play the call or any sound first.
  - Changed headset or speakers mid-call? Click Stop Listening, then Start Listening.
  - Your rebuttal library is saved in rebuttal_library.json next to the app.
  - Settings tab: pick a theme, change any color, set the text size and font, and set up
    the Suggested Rebuttal window. Click "Apply changes". Your transcript and highlights stay.
  - Highlight text in the transcript with a color and a note, then click Save Transcript.
    Saved files go in the "Saved transcripts" folder next to RebuttalButler.exe.
  - Recording is on by default and records both sides. The round red button at the top
    right turns recording on or off, even during a call. The Record dropdown beside it
    picks My side, Caller or Both (it is locked while recording).
    Recording starts with a boop-DING and stops with a BOOP-ding. Each recording is ONE
    MP3 in the "audiofiles" folder next to the app, saved as soon as you click Stop Listening.
    With Both, the file is stereo and both sides are in both the left and right channels,
    so you hear the caller and yourself in each ear.
  - The Recordings tab shows a live level meter for each side. Use the gain sliders (dB)
    to make a quiet side louder or softer in the recording. Gain changes the recording
    only, not the transcript. Below that, pick a saved recording to play it, and use the
    playback volume slider to change how loud it plays here.
    Recording customer calls: in Illinois and many other states everyone on the call must
    agree to be recorded. Tell the customer at the start of the call.
  - Click and recording sounds can be switched off, and their volume changed, on the
    Settings tab.
  - Your settings are saved in settings.json next to the app. Use "Export settings" on the
    Settings tab to keep a copy or move it to your other computer.

If something is wrong
  - Double-click CheckSetup.bat. It tests the speech engine and the audio capture
    and shows what it found.
  - mrai.log (and crash.txt, if the app crashed) sit next to the app. Send them along
    when asking for help.
